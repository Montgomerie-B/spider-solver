# Anytime whole-game controller v0.11

## Scope and result

v0.11 audits and corrects the existing bounded dependency-closure realiser for
a fresh named `SOURCE_BURIED` requirement. It adds no search engine and changes
no closure, controller, allocator, milestone, beam or benchmark resource
ceiling. The verified verdict is **PARTIAL**: generic and natural candidate
selection materially improved, but the cost-21 natural gate did not remove
foundation #2 or complete a source chain.

The exact v0.10 selected 2/3 residual is not committed as a complete explicit,
independently replayable artifact. Its diagnostic status is therefore
`NATURAL_RESIDUAL_ARTIFACT_UNAVAILABLE`; no state was guessed from hashes.

## v0.10 blocker and candidate autopsy

The exact graph and milestone adapter already identified the final buried
source. The closure generator attributed actions only when they directly moved
the dependency column/campaign material or satisfied a broad campaign rule.
It therefore missed legal off-column prerequisites such as exposing a receiver
by making a permanent same-suit join. A second defect dropped
`TacticalDemand.target_dependency_id` before closure and omitted that identity
from the cache key, allowing a source-targeted call to behave like an unrelated
campaign-wide call.

`buried_source_closure.py` now supplies proof-neutral typed facts:

- every physical source copy and current chosen copy;
- source zone, depth, ordered blockers and movable blocker groups;
- exact legal blocker moves and current destinations;
- candidate stage, disposition and typed rejection reason;
- receiver/workspace/park needs and complete lifecycle assessment;
- source-depth, blocker, dependency and copy-substitution progress;
- per-depth beam retention and discarded target-progress geometry;
- `SEARCH_POLICY`, `RESOURCE_BOUND`, `STRUCTURAL_BLOCKER` or local-miss
  diagnosis.

Audit records are bounded and never enter canonical identity or proof logic.
Diagnostic legal-coverage mode is disabled by default and does not change the
route chosen when enabled.

## Source progress and prerequisites

Fresh target progress includes source consumption, exposure, actionability,
depth/blocker reduction, receiver creation, workspace creation/use, bounded
temporary parking, permanent same-suit prerequisites and physical-copy
substitution. Receiver or workspace creation may remain eligible without an
immediate depth reduction. After every admitted primitive, closure independently
replays the action, rebuilds the exact graph, locates all interchangeable source
copies again and attaches progress to the semantic dependency rather than a
stale coordinate.

A temporary mixed/workspace park remains eligible only with a concrete named
target benefit and bounded exit. An exit that becomes legal by exposing the
source is recorded explicitly, with rehandling debt and compensating benefit.
Comparable permanent same-suit joins retain dominance. Breaking a stable join
requires named source-chain compensation and a bounded restore/replace route;
the evidence remains ordering-only.

The local beam remains width 192 by default. Within that existing width it
reserves bounded representatives of materially distinct target-progress kinds
before filling remaining positions by the existing priority. Exact structural
duplicates still retain only the lowest corrected `g`.

reductions into source exposure/consumption before the fresh milestone call

## Gate K decision and proof safety

None of the five authorization conditions held: no replayable v0.10 residual
was available, Gate J did not reach F2, substantial source-chain completion or
terminal qualification, and it did not execute a natural prerequisite chain
through named-source consumption. The 180-second untouched Gate K was therefore
not run.

Unrestricted Deal remains on. Exact TT remains `exact structural state ->
lowest corrected g`. Target identities, candidate traces, lifecycle debt,
beam history and failure diagnoses do not enter structural identity. The
admissible Deal/reveal bound is unchanged, and a bounded closure miss cannot
proof-prune the game.
