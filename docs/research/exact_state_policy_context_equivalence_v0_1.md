## 1. Verdict

suppression of this g=9 arrival is therefore correct for the observed case.

## 6. Equal-g causal result

The primary A2-versus-B1 comparison held g at 9 and was exactly equal. The
secondary A1-versus-B2 comparison held g at 7 and was also exactly equal. Native
A1-versus-B1 was equal as well. Consequently neither returned context nor the +2
spent cost changed selective coverage at this state; there is no context effect
and no independent g effect to explain away.

The returned context did change provenance-bearing investment/closure identities
inside some otherwise identical successors. Those are not genuine policy novelty:
they produced no new endpoint, action, family, obligation, continuation successor,
or source-completion trace. Merely different evidence IDs and labels do not satisfy
the audit's novelty definition.

## 7. Existing context-reconstruction result

Production's pop path indexes `completion_context_by_state` by canonical state key
and merges only `source_completion_ledger.satisfactions`. On TT suppression it can
reconstruct and save such satisfactions only when the rejected successor carries
`source_completion_traces`.

This return edge carried zero source-completion traces. Its dependency-closure
result, structural investment, and continuation credit are therefore not recovered
by the existing state-keyed store and are lost under TT suppression. That loss is
real telemetry/policy provenance loss, but the controlled generator comparison
shows it is capability-inert at this exact state. The verdict is therefore not
`CONTEXT_ALREADY_RECOVERED_BY_STATE`.

## 8. One-generation novelty test

Not performed. B1 exposed zero genuinely context-novel final successors, so the
conditional lookahead gate did not open. There was no returned-only successor from
which to run the prescribed additional expansion, and hence no returned-only
structural progress, face-down reduction, empty preservation/recovery, or campaign
edge progress one generation later.

## 9. Architectural implication

The distinction remains important in general: exact-state TT dominance governs
best game-state arrival, while selective expansion coverage could require separate
policy-context accounting. For this observed round trip, however, the supposedly
enriched policy context did not change coverage after g was controlled. Adding
context to the TT key, overriding admission, or changing the `(state, credit)`
expansion guard would retain a more expensive duplicate without demonstrated
search capability. No production fix is justified by this case.
