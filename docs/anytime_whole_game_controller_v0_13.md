# Anytime whole-game controller v0.13

## Scope and verdict

v0.13 adds proof-neutral tactical grant lineage for a semantic target across
fresh exact states, and audits the first fresh candidates, lifecycle
obligations, and strategic admission at that boundary. It does not add a
search engine, enlarge a grant, carry unused resources, change a benchmark
weight, seed a route, or begin the whole-deal scheduler. Unrestricted Deal
remains on.

The verified verdict is **PARTIAL**. The exact-state tier-reset hypothesis was
confirmed and corrected generically: an earned, still-relevant `SHALLOW` or
`COMMITTED` opportunity can now follow the same coordinate-free semantic
target into its next exact state. Natural Gate M exercised retained promotion
and executed a previously lost next-action class, authorizing Gate N under
condition 5. Gate M nevertheless retained only foundation #1 and Gate N
removed no foundation. Neither run exposed or consumed a named natural source,
so the correction has not yet converted into foundation #2.

## Allocator boundary audit

The required pre-policy audit found:

1. The v0.8 allocator evidence key is the exact canonical state plus objective,
   campaign, realiser, configuration, critical path, target dependency and
   terminal context.
2. A fresh demand begins at `PROBE`.
3. Named structural harvest recorded by the allocator can promote a later
   request in the same exact context.
4. That promotion evidence is deliberately tied to the exact structural
   state.
5. v0.10-v0.12 carry a coordinate-free semantic target and residual dependency
   across the outer milestone boundary.
6. They did not carry allocator evidence across that boundary.
7. Consequently, verified target-specific harvest was forgotten when the
   exact state changed.
8. A persisted target therefore restarted at `PROBE`, even after earning a
   stronger opportunity.
9. Exact-state isolation was deliberate protection against stale expenditure;
   its interaction with later semantic-target persistence was the accidental
   mismatch.
10. The unchanged per-expansion ceilings remain 12,000 tactical nodes and four
    tactical seconds. Gate M and N also retain their controller, expansion,
    tactical-node, frontier, beam and local-closure ceilings.

## Target grant lineage

`TargetGrantLineage` maps coordinate-free semantic target fingerprints to
`TargetGrantLineageEntry` records. Each entry records campaign/objective and
dependency identity, prior and current exact states and blockers, tier
requests and grants, named harvest, completion class, source depth and blocker
changes, prerequisite and substantial progress, resource consumption,
lifecycle debt and restore/replace obligation, validity, status, transition
reason, misses, generation and step count. `TargetCommitmentEvidence`,
`TargetGrantDecision`, and `TargetBoundaryTrace` make evidence and every
boundary decision inspectable.

Lineage is target-specific, not coordinate-specific. It is rebuilt or attached
only after exact TT admission and never enters canonical Spider identity.
Different targets and campaigns cannot inherit one another's evidence, and
commitment follows the target rather than a particular physical source copy or
tactical realiser.

## Retention, promotion, decay and reset

A fresh target without named harvest remains `PROBE`. A valid same target may
retain or promote its next bounded opportunity only from portable target
evidence such as dependency advancement, source-depth or blocker reduction,
named prerequisite completion, receiver/workspace fulfilment, copy
substitution, or substantial target progress. Unrelated progress, elapsed
time, node count and generic joins do not promote it. Existing v0.8 promotion
rules cap inherited commitment at `COMMITTED`; `TERMINAL` still requires the
fresh unchanged terminal predicate.

Repeated no-harvest misses demote or reset the entry. Contradictory fresh
analysis, invalidation, supersession, materially unrelated blockers,
uncompensated lifecycle debt and the bounded persistence envelope also reset,
invalidate or expire it. A carried tier applies only to the next opportunity;
it is not sunk-cost protection and does not force the controller to pursue the
target.

No grant balance is stored. A new request receives one existing tier's normal
cost/node/time tranche, subject to the unchanged per-expansion and whole-run
limits. `PROBE`, `SHALLOW`, `COMMITTED` and `TERMINAL` specifications are
unchanged, as are the dependency-closure beam and limits.

## Fresh-candidate, lifecycle and admission audit

Every persisted-target boundary trace records the old and new exact states,
semantic target and residual dependency, blocker and physical source, prior
harvest and grant consumption, lifecycle debt, the fresh relevant candidate
set and classes, requested/granted tier, whether commitment was inherited,
the best candidate and whether it was inside the grant, execution,
admission/rejection and resulting progress.

Failures are classified as `TACTICAL_TIER_RESET`,
`FRESH_CANDIDATE_TURNOVER`, `TARGET_ATTRIBUTION_LOSS`,
`LIFECYCLE_MISORDERING`, `STRATEGIC_ADMISSION_LOSS`, `RESOURCE_BOUND`,
`STRUCTURAL_BLOCKER`, `TARGET_SUPERSEDED`, `EXPIRED`, or an explicit fallback
when the recorded evidence is genuinely insufficient.

A still-valid stable-run restore/replace obligation follows the same target;
fresh analysis can retain, improve, worsen, replace or drop it. It is not part
of exact state identity. The selected policy keeps all normal conservative,
raw, Deal and construction alternatives, while reserving at most one frontier
representative for the strongest actionable same-target descendant with
portable harvest. Exact lower-cost TT dominance still decides admission before
any planning context is attached.

## Capability gates and unseen deals

All generic Gates A-K pass. They cover earned `SHALLOW` retention over an exact
state change; no-harvest reset; blocker-changing continuity; target/campaign
isolation; bounded decay; fresh-only terminal qualification; legal progression
through source depth and exposure; candidate-turnover diagnosis;
restore/replace persistence; bounded admission continuity with a conservative
alternative; and unchanged exact TT dominance and context rebuilding.

The focused v0.13 file contains 60 test functions and 65 parameterised cases.
The combined v0.8-v0.13 controller cohort passes 385 cases. Two deterministic
unseen four-suit smokes retained unrestricted rules, the raw fallback family,
Deal and construction coverage, and replay-valid bounded results while
exercising target lineage.

## Natural Gate M

Gate M started at the independently replayed cost-21 machine F1 checkpoint.
Its limits were exactly 90 seconds, 25 strategic expansions, 300,000 tactical
nodes, frontier 256 and dependency-closure beam 192, with all tier and closure
tranches unchanged. It reached the 25-expansion ceiling in about 23.4 seconds
and used roughly 220 closure nodes.

The selected five-action suffix added corrected `g=5`, made no Deal, and ended
at total `g=26`, one Spade foundation, stock 30 and 32 face-down cards. It
independently replayed with path hash `18843bfb94399fdb`, endpoint hash
`30496cf7f013e61f`, and structural hash `31659973bc6dba50`.

The explored frontier contained retained `SHALLOW` and `COMMITTED`
opportunities and executed a next target-action class that v0.12's fresh
`PROBE` boundary lost. This was the concrete condition-5 authorization for
Gate N. Advanced descendants were admitted without displacing the normal
conservative fill. The run still recorded zero actual named source exposures,
zero consumptions, zero substantial source-chain completions, zero terminal
qualifications and no F2. Trace-level `SOURCE_EXPOSED`/completion predicates
must not be confused with those zero controller-level natural source events.

## Untouched Gate N

Gate N was authorized only by the demonstrated boundary correction above. It
started from the true untouched deal with no incumbent, prefix, suit, source,
candidate or canonical action. Its limits remained exactly 180 seconds, 50
strategic expansions, 500,000 tactical nodes, frontier 256 and beam 192.

The run reached all 50 expansions. It produced two substantial interval
milestones and purposeful, legality-audited stock transitions with fresh
post-Deal analysis, but no substantial source-chain completion, actual named
source exposure/consumption, terminal qualification, F1 or F2. Because F2 was
absent, deterministic F2 repeat, optional F3 and optional whole-game runs were
not authorized.

## Regression, proof safety and genericity

The canonical 172 route, machine cost-21 F1 and independent cost-23 F1 anchors
remain independently replay-valid with their established command counts,
stock/foundation/face-down facts and hashes. The complete-suite result is
**1,244 ordinary passes, 37 expected historical xfails and the single inherited
warning in 1,146.43 seconds**. No xfail was weakened.

Exact TT remains `exact structural state -> lowest corrected g`. Lineage,
tier, lifecycle and admission metadata are added only after admission and have
no proof authority. The admissible Deal/reveal lower bound and all pruning
semantics are unchanged. Production policy contains no benchmark path/hash,
fixed suit/rank/column, external 119 score or prospective canonical action.

## Precise blocker and next task

The exact-state tier-reset defect is corrected, but the unchanged natural
persistence and resource envelope still expires or turns over same-target
work before actual named-source exposure. Gate M and Gate N show retained
opportunities and interval progress, yet selected natural chains do not turn
that continuity into source exposure, terminal qualification or F2.

If a later task is explicitly authorized, it should trace the remaining
source-scoped gap between an executed retained candidate and controller-level
named-source exposure: distinguish legitimate expiry from fresh candidate
turnover and lost physical-source attribution, and verify whether the current
three-boundary persistence envelope ends one step too early for already-named
progress. It must not enlarge any tier or global limit, reward sunk cost, tune
the benchmark, or begin a global scheduler.
